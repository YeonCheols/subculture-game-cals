## Discovery queries

Use domain-restricted searches when a news index is dynamic or difficult to enumerate:

- `site:mongil.netmarble.com 몬길 이벤트 OR 업데이트 OR 점검`
- `site:wutheringwaves.kurogames.com 명조 이벤트 OR 업데이트 OR 점검`
- `site:genshin.hoyoverse.com/ko/news 원신 이벤트 OR 업데이트 OR 기원`

Add a version number, event name, or a recent date to narrow results. Search-result snippets are discovery hints only.

## Source acceptance

Accept a record as `confirmed` only when its timing and identity are supported by an opened official page. Use `probable` when an official source confirms the item but a required time field is ambiguous. Use `unverified` only for leads kept outside user-facing schedules.
